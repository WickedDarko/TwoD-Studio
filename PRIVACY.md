# Privacy Policy — TwoD Studio for Android

This policy describes the Android app, TwoD Studio version 1.6.2 (build 29).

## Information handled by the app

TwoD Studio does not require an account. The Android app has no advertising, analytics, or cloud-sync feature. It does not automatically upload your projects, artwork, imported files, audio, or exports.

Projects and drafts are stored locally on your device. The app also stores a local preference recording that you acknowledged the first-launch privacy notice.

## Files, media, and permissions

The app accesses images, project files, and audio when you choose an import or open action. It uses Android's system file or media picker so you choose which items to provide; it does not request broad storage access at startup.

Audio playback uses audio you select. The app does not record sound and does not need microphone access.

## Exports

Exports are created on your device and saved to the **Download/TwoD Studio Export** folder. You control what you do with those files afterward, including whether to move or share them using other apps or services.

## External services

If you choose to open an external link, such as Buy Me a Coffee, or contact the developer through an email app, the selected website or email provider handles information you provide there under its own practices. TwoD Studio does not control those services.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact **twod_studio@proton.me**.
